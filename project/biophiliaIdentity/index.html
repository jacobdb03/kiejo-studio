This project explored the creation of a full identity for an exhibition called Biophilia, which explored the connection between plants and people and the human need for nature in their daily lives.

Being a longer project, it allowed me to learn about the process and lifecycle of a bigger project, and to continue developing new technologies and abstract methods of communication.
