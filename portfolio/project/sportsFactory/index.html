Sports Factory's brand.

How did Sports Factory come to look the way it does?

This rebrand required a minimalistic black and white identity that would stand out among brands with similar missions. The logomark has subtle meanings to both sport and religion, indicating the two primary goals of the charity.

What excited me the most about this project was that it was my first time working with a client outside of uni.

Sports Factory needs to work across various school and team colours.
